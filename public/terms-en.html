3. Encryption

All sensitive data (sighting dates, separation days, periods) is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from the user's password.

About Encryption:

Data is protected with strong encryption (AES-256-GCM) and is not accessible via admin interface, not saved in logs, and not visible during normal service operation.
